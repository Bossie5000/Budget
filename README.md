# Monthly Budget Tracker 💰

A comprehensive web-based budget tracking application to manage your monthly finances, including income, expenses, fuel purchases, and account balances.

## 🌟 Features

- **Income Tracking**: Track multiple income sources
- **Fuel Advance Management**: Monitor fuel purchases and advances
- **Expense Categories**: 
  - Fixed Expenses
  - Flexible Expenses
  - Subscriptions
  - International Bank Fees
  - Ad Hoc Expenses
- **Account Management**: Track credit and savings account balances
- **Monthly Summary**: Comprehensive financial overview
- **Data Import/Export**: Save and restore your budget data
- **Multi-Month Support**: Manage budgets across different months

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/yourusername/your-repo-name.git
   ```

2. Open `index.html` in your web browser

That's it! The app runs entirely in your browser - no server or installation needed.

## 📖 How to Use

1. **Select or Create a Month**: Use the month selector to choose your current month or add a new one
2. **Add Income**: Navigate to the Income tab and add your income sources
3. **Track Expenses**: Use the different expense category tabs to add and manage expenses
4. **Monitor Fuel**: Track fuel purchases and advances in the Fuel tab
5. **Manage Accounts**: Update your credit and savings account balances
6. **View Summary**: Check your monthly summary and balance checker

### Data Persistence
Your data is stored locally in your browser's storage. To backup or transfer your data:
- Use **Export Data** button to save your budget to a JSON file
- Use **Import Data** button to restore from a previously saved file

## 🔒 Privacy

All data is stored locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. Your financial information stays on your device.
